About us

A village group, run by the village

We are volunteers from Moygashel who work together to improve the place we live. Our logo shows the old mill chimney with a flax flower, a lily and a poppy, a nod to the village's linen heritage and to the people who have made it what it is.

Recent projects include communal area clean-ups, a winter safety initiative, an elderly winter lighting project, an electrical safety initiative, community fun days and educational day trips.

Each new project is a chance for neighbours to get involved, whether that means picking up a tool, bringing a flask of tea or sharing an idea.

  • Duty

    Turning up and doing the work that needs doing.

  • Loyalty

    Standing by the village and the people who live in it.

  • Community

    Open to everyone who wants to lend a hand.
